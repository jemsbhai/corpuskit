# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CorpusKit a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.

Harassment, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, trolling, and disclosure of private
information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `jemsbhai@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and fairly, with confidentiality respected as far as practical. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants
whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
